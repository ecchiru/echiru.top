# About Me

My name is Ethyl Acetate (乙酸乙酯), but you can call me YiYi(乙乙)~
Why did I choose this name? Perhaps it comes from my deep obsession with chemistry.
The first long chemical formula I ever encountered was CH₃COOC₂H₅. It has the scent of green apples, yet it's flammable and explosive, with low toxicity. In a way, it reflects my personality in a unique way...
Here, I'll be recording the interesting things I come across, some thoughts on building this site, and… occasionally losing my mind (crossed out).
Updates are irregular, but if you like this place, feel free to check in often! 🥺
